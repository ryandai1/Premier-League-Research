## Findings and disclosure

Rounded scores remain reproducible under fixed deterministic settings. The independent Arsenal and league arms have different learned weights and predictions, with a maximum absolute test-probability difference of about 0.254. Previous repeated-execution bars were removed; the new result graphic compares model arms within one execution. Equality across corresponding reruns is not equality between control and treatment.

The test period was previously inspected. This is explicitly a **reproducibility rerun**, with no new confirmatory claim. Unequal optimizer-update budgets, one seed, incomplete original-source lineage and the small test sample remain disclosed. Repository visibility was not changed.
