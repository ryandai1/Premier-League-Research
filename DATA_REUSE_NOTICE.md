This repository grants no broader data rights. Do not treat this as clearance for commercial use or unrestricted redistribution.

Modifications in this project: auditable cleaned training tables from the supplied release, separate corrections to inconsistent season metadata, feature lineage, experimental models, and analysis. Raw source bytes are preserved. Corrections are computational reconciliations, not newly observed historical facts. See EXPERIMENT_PROTOCOL.md for exact changes and limitations.
